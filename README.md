# Professional Services SOW Revenue Leakage

Capture unbilled services, milestones, expenses, scope changes, and contractual price adjustments.

**Primary buyer:** Consulting, implementation, and managed-service firms. **Evidence:** MSAs, SOWs, milestones, resources, time, expenses, deliverables, change requests, acceptance, invoices, and collections.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- MSA SOW library
- Resource rate card
- Time entry ingestion
- Expense eligibility
- Milestone completion evidence
- Deliverable acceptance tracking
- Fixed-fee allocation
- Time-material billing
- Change request detection
- Out-of-scope work quantification
- Price escalation control
- Invoice generation
- Client dispute workflow
- Cash collection tracking
- Project client analytics
